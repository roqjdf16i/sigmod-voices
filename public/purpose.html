OUR PURPOSE

For the community.
For better science.

We care about SIGMOD because we care about the future of database research.

Our field flourishes when promising ideas can be heard, standards are understood, and researchers can question the processes that shape their work. Transparent, consistent, and thoughtful review practices strengthen both scientific rigor and the trust that makes a research community possible.

We believe SIGMOD's current desk rejection process leaves too much room for arbitrary decisions. Authors deserve clear criteria, consistent treatment, and substantive explanations when their work is rejected without a full review. We also welcome broader perspectives on SIGMOD, peer review, and life in the database community. We seek to make room for constructive reflection while respecting the work of reviewers and organizers. We recognize the demands of reviewing at scale. We also believe that listening to authors, including those whose work does not reach review, is essential to improving that process.

Our aim is to help the database community learn, evolve, and thrive. We want to nurture intellectual diversity, support researchers at the beginning of their careers, and build conditions in which excellent scholarship can flourish. We welcome thoughtful disagreement, concrete experiences, and perspectives shared in good faith.
